About these numbers

What is counted

Course registrations are summed across sections and recorded quarters. They do not measure unique students, class size, room capacity, or faculty workload.

How years are compared

The program confirms a cap of 20 students per section as of 2026. Captured EagleNET capacities are preserved as recorded.

Academic years begin in Fall. A prior-year comparison uses the same recorded quarters in both years. A partial year is never compared with a full year. Comparisons involving provisional registration counts are withheld.

Automatic quarter

The automatic view uses EWU term dates and Pacific time. During breaks it focuses the upcoming quarter. Outside the published date range it uses a labeled seasonal estimate. You can select another quarter without changing the academic year.
