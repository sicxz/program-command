About these numbers

What is counted

Course registrations are summed across sections and recorded quarters. They do not measure unique students, class size, room capacity, or faculty workload.

How years are compared

Academic years begin in Fall. A prior-year comparison uses the same recorded quarters in both years. A partial year is never compared with a full year.
