About these numbers

Registration sources

Historical enrollment and complete EagleNET captures use the same source rules as Enrollment. A capture replaces the same quarter’s older counts. Fall 2026 remains provisional until a reviewed capture replaces it.

Enrollment · Registration captures

Supervision sources

A saved schedule or import replaces historical workload for its academic year, even when only one quarter has records. Faculty detail entries are appended. The view does not estimate student counts from credit values or use the legacy summary’s assumed student counts.

Period and coverage

All recorded years is the default. The same academic year, quarter and course filters apply to both measures. Missing supervision records stay unavailable, even where registrations exist.

The automatic quarter uses EWU’s calendar in Pacific time and focuses the upcoming term during breaks. Outside known dates, the term is labeled as an estimate.
