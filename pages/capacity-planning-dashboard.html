About these numbers

Course load and targets

Teaching load comes from saved course records. Applied-learning courses use their configured workload multipliers. AY Setup supplies faculty roles, annual targets and releases.

Adjunct and unassigned teaching count toward demand. They do not add to the permanent full-time teaching target.

Coverage and comparisons

Saved course records can cover only part of a year and have no completeness guarantee. A target minus partial course records is not confirmed available capacity. Individual overload is the sum of full-time assignments above each annual net target.

The automatic quarter follows EWU’s calendar in Pacific time, focusing the upcoming term during breaks. Outside known dates it uses a labeled seasonal estimate.
